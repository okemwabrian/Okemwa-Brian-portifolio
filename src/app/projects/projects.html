Engineering highlights

One connected control room

  1. Multi-tenant access controlSandboxed Client, Driver, Dispatcher, and System Administrator portals; sign in with a username or generated OF-XXXXXX account ID.
  2. Deterministic shipment lifecyclePending Review → Confirmed → Assigned → In-Transit → Delivered. Drivers can record active trip logs but cannot authorize delivery sign-off.
  3. Automated double-entry ledgerQuote approval creates a pro-forma invoice; delivery confirmation creates the final invoice and balanced Accounts Receivable / Freight Revenue entries.
  4. Live telemetry and documentsHTMX tracking updates without page reloads, with generated bills of lading, waybills, and delivery notes.
  5. Append-only audit trailPrintable records capture operational actions, tariff changes, and driver assignments.
